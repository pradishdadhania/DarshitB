About Bharat Workforce Solutions

We are a Vadodara-based integrated industrial and infrastructure services company. Manufacturers, project owners and warehouse operators struggle with unprofessional vendors, high compliance risk and juggling many contractors. We were founded to solve exactly that, with civil, electrical and structural engineers, warehouse experts, manpower specialists and corporate advisors (CA, CS, audit) working together.

We don't just supply manpower. We deliver professional, compliant and expert-led execution.

Our vision

To be the most trusted and compliant service partner for manufacturing industries, infrastructure projects and the warehousing sector across India.

Our mission

To let clients focus on core growth while we manage manpower, engineering, maintenance, warehouse operations and compliance with professionalism, statutory integrity and expert-led solutions.
